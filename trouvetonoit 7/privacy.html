Politique de confidentialité

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who controls votre data

Trouve Ton Toit, operating en France, is the data controller pour the personal data described in this policy. For any privacy question, see our Contact page.

2. What we collect

CategoryExamples

3. Why we collect it

  • To provide the service — matching locataires avec propriétaires, generating bails, processing applications
  • Identity verification — so propriétaires can trust the applicants they avis
  • Paiements — processing platform fees et , pour agences, subscriptions, via Stripe
  • Legal compliance — français bail law requires certain identifying information on the bail itself
  • Communication — enabling messages between propriétaires, locataires et agences

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

We process personal data on the basis of: performance of a contract (providing the location-matching service you signed up pour ), legal obligation (bail registration requirements), et consent (e.g. optional marketing communications, where applicable).

5. Who we share data avec

  • The other party to a transaction (e.g. a propriétaire sees a locataire's dossier once they apply; a locataire sees propriétaire identification once required pour a bail)
  • Service providers we rely on to operate the platform: Supabase (database/storage), Clerk (authentication), Stripe (paiements), DocuSeal (e-signatures), Resend (transactional email)
  • Authorities, where required by law (e.g. bail registration avec le Housing Authority is the propriétaire's own responsibility, not something Trouve Ton Toit submits on their behalf)

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

6. How long we keep it

We retain compte et location data pour as long as votre compte is actif, et pour a reasonable period afterward to comply avec legal, tax or dispute-resolution obligations. Identity documents are retained only as long as necessary pour verification et dossier purposes.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export votre personal data, et to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us via the Contact page. You also have the right to lodge a complaint avec France's Information et Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC).

8. Cookies

Details on cookies et similar technologies are covered separately in our Politique de cookies.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted storage, access controls, secure paiement processing via Stripe) to protect personal data, though no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy depuis time to time; the "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
