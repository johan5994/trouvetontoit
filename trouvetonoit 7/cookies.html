Politique de cookies

Last updated: August 2026

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on votre device when you visite a website. They help the site function, remember votre preferences, et — where used — understand how visitors use the site.

2. Cookies we use

TypePurposeConsent required?
EssentialKeeping you logged in, remembering votre session, basic site functionalityNo — required pour the site to work
PreferencesRemembering paramètres such as language or display choicesNo, or implied
AnalyticsUnderstanding how visitors use the site, to improve itYes, where used

Trouve Ton Toit does not currently use third-party advertising cookies.

3. Third-party services

Some features rely on third-party providers that may set their own cookies or use similar technologies when their services load in votre browser, dont Clerk (authentication), Stripe (paiements) et Leaflet/OuvrirStreetMap (maps). Each operates under its own privacy et cookie practices.

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through votre browser paramètres. Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of Trouve Ton Toit — such as staying logged in — depuis working correctly.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the cookies we use change. See our Politique de confidentialité pour how we handle personal data more broadly.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies can be sent via our Contact page.
