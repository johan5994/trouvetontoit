A few things worth explaining

Trouve Ton Toit does more than a typical annonces site — here's the "why" behind the parts that aren't obvious at first glance.

📸 What's the "entrée dans les lieux état des lieux"?

Formally called an État des lieux (état des lieux) in français location practice — a room-by-room record of the bien's condition et both meter readings, photographed et signed by both parties the day the locataire moves in. Its whole purpose is to settle disputes fairly later — if something's disputed at move-out, there's an agreed, dated record depuis entrée dans les lieux to compare against.

🪪 Why does Trouve Ton Toit need my ID?

Two reasons. First, trust — a propriétaire reviewing votre application, or a locataire reviewing who they're renting depuis , can see a vérifié identity instead of just a name in a chat. Second, français bails legally require each party's identification to appear on the document itself, so it's needed either way.

💶 What's the platform fee actually pour ?

It covers running the verification, e-signing et hosting behind the platform. It's only ever charged if a locataire's application is actually accepté — never upfront, et never if you're refusé. Biens managed by an agence don't carry this fee at all, since agences pay their own separate subscription instead.

🏢 Do I need an agence, or can I self-manage?

Self-managing directly between propriétaire et locataire is the default et works pour most people — Trouve Ton Toit handles the bail, e-signing et entrée dans les lieux état des lieux either way. An agence is there pour propriétaires who'd rather hand off the day-to-day (visites, applications, coordination) entirely.

⚖️ Is the bail actually legally valid?

Bails generated through Trouve Ton Toit are built to comply avec France's Private Residential Bails Act (Chapter 604) — covering the bien address, agreed use, duration, loyer et paiement method, dépôt de garantie, et the entrée dans les lieux état des lieux as required by law. It remains the propriétaire's responsibility to register the bail avec le Housing Authority within the legally required timeframe.

🔑 What happens to my dépôt de garantie et loyer?

They're never held by Trouve Ton Toit. Once a bail is signed, dépôt de garantie et first month's loyer go straight depuis the locataire's paiement method to the propriétaire's own compte — the platform only ever facilitates the transaction, it isn't a party to it.
