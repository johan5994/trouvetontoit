Conditions Générales d'Utilisation

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

Trouve Ton Toit ("we", "us", "the platform") is a plateforme locative connecting propriétaires, locataires, immobilier agences et independent agents en France. Trouve Ton Toit facilitates annonces, communication, visite scheduling, bail generation et entrée dans les lieux inventories — it is not a party to any contrat de bail signed through the platform.

2. Accounts

You must provide accurate information when creating an compte et keep votre login credentials confidential. You are responsible pour all activity under votre compte. Trouve Ton Toit may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these terms, provide false information, or engage in fraudulent behaviour.

3. Annonces

Propriétaires et agences are solely responsible pour the accuracy of their annonces, dont price, availability, photos et description. Trouve Ton Toit does not verify bien ownership or the legal right to bail a bien, though it may request supporting documents at its discretion.

4. Identity verification

Locataires may be asked to submit identity documents et supporting paperwork to build a location dossier. This information is used solely to help propriétaires assess applications et is handled in accordance avec our Politique de confidentialité.

5. Paiements et fees

Where Trouve Ton Toit charges a platform fee, this is disclosed to the locataire before any paiement is authorised, et is only captured if an application is accepté by the propriétaire. Loyer et dépôt de garanties are paid directly between locataire et propriétaire; Trouve Ton Toit does not hold or manage these funds. Immobilier agences operate under a separate subscription arrangement.

6. Bails et legal compliance

Bails generated through the platform are intended to comply avec le Private Residential Bails Act (Chapter 604 of the Laws of France). It remains the propriétaire's responsibility to register the bail avec le Housing Authority within the legally required timeframe, et both parties' responsibility to ensure the final signed bail reflects their actual agreement.

7. Prohibited conduct

  • Posting false, misleading or duplicate annonces
  • Circumventing the platform to avoid applicable fees after being introduced through it
  • Harassment, discrimination or abusive behaviour toward other users
  • Upchargement falsified identity or financial documents

8. Limitation of liability

Trouve Ton Toit provides tools to facilitate location transactions but is not responsible pour the conduct of propriétaires, locataires, agences or agents, nor pour the condition of any bien listed. Disputes arising depuis a bail are between the contracting parties.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms depuis time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

10. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent via our Contact page.
